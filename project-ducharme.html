DUCHARME SEATING

Large-scale venue visualization

Partnered with Ducharme Seating to build an extensive digital catalog for their premium auditorium and theater seating. The project encompassed high-precision product modeling and large-scale architectural environmental visual integration for world-class venues.

Developed a modular 3D pipeline to efficiently render diverse upholstery and wood finish combinations, empowering sales teams with versatile imagery for commercial bidding.

Architected complex venue environments featuring thousands of high-poly seating components, preserving photorealistic raytraced lighting and tactile material fidelity.

Produced technical motion and spatial animations demonstrating seat kinematics, spatial layout, and sightlines to pitch venue architectural layouts.
