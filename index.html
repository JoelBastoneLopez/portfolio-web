About Me & Methodology

Professional background, collaborative approach, and workflow.

I am a 3D product artist with over 7 years of experience. I work with brands and agencies that need photo-quality images and experiences without the cost and logistics of a physical studio. I model, texture, and render the product once, and that same model powers catalog images, animations for ads, and interactive web viewers (Three.js). I work from Buenos Aires (GMT-3), offering great overlap with US time zones.

01

Brief & Scope

We define what you need to sell, the reference materials (CAD, samples), and pieces required. Fixed quote and timeline provided.

02

3D Model & Look Test

Before full production, you approve materials and lighting on a test render. Course-correct when it's still cheap.

03

Production

Catalog images, lifestyle scenes, animations, and web viewers all generated from the same core model.

04

Delivery & Scale

Optimized files with review rounds included. New colors and SKUs can be generated in a fraction of the time.
