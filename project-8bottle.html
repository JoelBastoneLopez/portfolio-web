8BOTTLE

Connected IoT Hardware — Visual Identity & Global Launch Campaign

Comprehensive 3D visual development, procedural technical schematics, and commercial launch ecosystem for a connected smart bottle — designed to highlight internal IoT features, drive high-volume pre-orders, and secure global cobranding partnerships with FIFA World Cup Qatar and Orangetheory Fitness.

Role Lead 3D CGI Artist & Visual Identity Designer
Client 8Bottle Inc. (San Francisco / Global)
Sector Connected Consumer IoT & Premium Lifestyle
Year 2023–2024
Duration 10 weeks
Stack Cinema 4D · Redshift · Substance 3D · After Effects · Photoshop
Deliverables Full CGI launch campaign, C4D procedural blueprint teardown, PBR droplet lookdev, 7 technical infographics, cobranding editions
01

CONTEXT

8Bottle was conceived as the next generation of connected hydration hardware — a premium smart bottle engineered with an integrated micro-LED capacitive touch display, algorithmic sip-tracking sensors, and a 15-day battery powered via inductive wireless charging.

With the physical product still locked in engineering prototypes and tooling phases, the brand faced a high-stakes market entry: they needed to launch a global crowdfunding campaign, pitch international retail distributors, and negotiate high-profile cobranding partnerships (including limited editions for the FIFA World Cup Qatar and Orangetheory Fitness) without a single manufactured production unit in hand.

02

THE PROBLEM

A product cannot sell on technical specifications alone — it requires compelling 3D visuals engineered to seduce buyers, prove innovation, and close commercial deals before tooling begins.

Crowdfunding backers, retail buyers, and brand partners evaluate consumer tech with their eyes in the first few seconds of scroll. Generic 3D renders placed on sterile studio backdrops fail to communicate what makes the product smart: the capacitive LED interaction, the vacuum-insulated thermal retention, the tactile grip of powder-coated steel, and the ergonomics of daily athletic use.

Without physical units for commercial photography, every micro-detail — from condensation beads forming on cold steel to optical light diffusion through the cap's LED array — had to be synthesized digitally with uncompromising fidelity.

03

OBJECTIVES

Three hard commercial and technical targets framed the visual pipeline:

0 Units Manufactured production units in hand at project kickoff — 100% synthetic visual launch
15-Day Battery life & wireless inductive charging system visualized through exploded 3D architecture
6 Channels Single master 3D pipeline powering Crowdfunding, Retail Decks, FIFA/Gym Cobranding & Social
04

CONSTRAINTS

Zero Photographic References

Every material property (double-wall vacuum stainless steel, food-grade silicone seals, soft-touch matte powder coat) had to be calibrated procedurally within Cinema 4D and Redshift without physical production samples.

Capacitive LED Sub-surface Diffusion

The smart cap features micro-LED notifications hidden beneath a semi-translucent polymer shell. The shader had to accurately replicate physical light scattering, falloff, and ambient contrast.

Multi-Brand Partnership Integrity

Cobranding integrations for the FIFA World Cup Qatar and Orangetheory Fitness demanded strict Pantone color matching, logo displacement mapping, and premium brand alignment.

Physics-Driven Condensation

To convey active cold hydration without physical moisture, hundreds of thousands of micro-droplets had to be distributed across curved surfaces using procedural particle clumping and meniscus surface tension.

05

PROCESS

Procedural Blueprint Shaders & Internal Structure

Engineered custom non-photorealistic blueprint shaders in Cinema 4D to visualize internal thermal insulation, wall thicknesses, and sensor placement for high-impact social and technical brand explainers.

Advanced PBR Material System

Engineered Redshift shaders replicating high-durability electrostatic powder coating with micro-grain bump, brushed stainless steel inner lining, and silicone gasket compliance.

Procedural Meniscus & Droplet LookDev

Developed dual-layer procedural displacement and particle scattering to simulate realistic condensation droplets with Fresnel refraction and internal reflection.

Technical Infographics & Feature Staging

Engineered 7 high-clarity technical breakdown plates and infographic diagrams detailing sensor telemetry, capacitive touch feedback, wireless charging, and hydration reminders.

06

KEY DECISIONS

The '1 Master 3D Asset to 6 Channels' Architecture

Built a unified 3D staging rig where changing the bottle's colorway or corporate logo automatically propagates across hero stills, studio macros, e-commerce whites, and technical infographic plates.

Procedural Droplet Physics over Texture Baking

Instead of painting static wet maps, droplets were generated as physical geometry instances, preserving true optical caustic highlights under dynamic studio lighting.

Cobranding as a Growth Engine

Rendered high-impact commemorative editions for the FIFA World Cup Qatar and Orangetheory Fitness, providing the sales team with turnkey mockups that unlocked partnership conversations.

Functional Visual Storytelling

Composed feature-led visual staging inspired by premium tech benchmark Larq, highlighting the rhythmic glow of the hydration reminder LED and the seamless magnetic wireless charging puck.
