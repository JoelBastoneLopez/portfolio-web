01

CONTEXT & THE BRIEF

Building a tactile, believable 3D universe for faction-based digital collectibles.

During the peak of the digital collectibles and avatar generation era, MWO contacted me to lead the 3D asset production for their universe. Characters were categorized into distinct narrative factions—ranging from high-tech cybernetic operatives and underground scavengers to alchemical engineers. The client provided initial 2D concept sketches and broad art direction notes. My mission was to translate flat line drawings into volumetric, high-fidelity 3D assets that would feel tangible, functional, and seamlessly swappable across all character base bodies without clipping.

02

RESEARCH & REFERENCE CURATION

Grounding stylized concepts in real-world engineering and tactile materiality.

2D sketches communicate silhouette and mood, but they often leave mechanical joints, rear angles, and fabric construction unresolved. Before opening 3D software, I spearheaded an extensive visual research phase. I curated dedicated reference sheets for each asset type, drawing from real industrial hydraulics for cybernetic limbs, military webbing and Cordura leather for utility belts, vintage aviator goggles for ocular gear, and technical glassware for energy capsules. This step ensured that every faction had an authentic design language that resonated with collectors.

03

PRODUCTION OBJECTIVES

Four architectural principles governed the modeling and asset delivery pipeline:

04

SCULPTING & HARD-SURFACE PIPELINE

A hybrid workflow blending ZBrush digital sculpting with precision Cinema 4D and Blender modeling.

Character assets require two fundamentally different skill sets: organic drapery that responds to gravity, and rigid mechanical parts that look industrially manufactured. To achieve both, I deployed a hybrid software pipeline:

Digital Sculpting in ZBrush

Used for cloth wrinkles, tactical sling bags, tension folds along strap seams, and leather pouch elasticity over anatomical mannequins. Panel loops and dynamic sub-divisions ensured crisp seam definitions.

Precision Hard-Surface in C4D & Blender

Used for robotic limbs, Kunai blades, tactical eye gear, and modular helmets. Prioritized clean edge flow, weighted bevels, and mechanical pivot alignment for realistic joint articulation.

Standardized Coordinate Origins

Every trait was authored around identical world pivots (neck socket, belt waistline, shoulder anchor) allowing programmatic assembly scripts to compose avatars automatically.

Topology & Polygroup Discipline

Meshes were organized with clear polygroups and material IDs, making secondary colorway iterations and texture map assignments effortless.

05

TEXTURING & LOOKDEV IN BLENDER

Crafting tactile PBR shader networks that celebrate every scratch, reflection, and glow.

Once high-poly modeling and retopology were locked, texturing and LookDev were developed inside Blender. To make each asset pop on digital marketplace cards and full-screen renders, I authored nuanced physical shader networks:

Weathered Leather & Fabrics

Micro-roughness variations, subtle surface creasing, and edge wear highlighting the handmade quality of scavenger faction gear.

Industrial Metals & Machining

Anodized coatings with faint hairline scratches, radial brush marks on cylinder ends, and realistic metallic reflection falloff.

Glass Refraction & Energy Volumes

Full transmission glass shaders with internal electric filament geometry and emissive bloom for alchemical power capsules.

Studio Presentation Lighting

Clean 3-point rim and key lighting setups against neutral deep-space dark backdrops, ensuring asset silhouettes read immediately.

07

INTEROPERABILITY & DELIVERY

Ensuring smooth ingestion into procedural generation engines and web viewers.

Creating 30+ standalone 3D models is only half the battle in a modular character ecosystem; the real technical challenge is guaranteed interoperability. Every single model was audited against the project’s master mannequin rig to confirm consistent scale, rotation axes, and zero penetration across overlapping accessories (e.g. wearing a bandana underneath goggles while carrying a robotic arm over a heavy leather belt).

Universal OBJ Geometry

Preserving high-density quad topology and explicit smoothing groups for offline marketing renders and print collateral.

Real-Time GLB & WebGL Ready

Exported with packed PBR textures (BaseColor, Metallic-Roughness, Normal, Emissive) for real-time web viewers and metaverse avatar compositors.

Non-Destructive Layering

Every piece designed with strict clearance envelopes, ensuring that an avatar generated with 5 simultaneous traits never suffers clipping.

Turnkey Asset Hand-off

Comprehensive naming convention and folder structure delivered to the dev team for automated batch rendering scripts.
